Establishment

Established during the national lockdown 2020. We have seen the improtance of intergrating online learing measures like those used in higher education in Secondary Education.

We have made it our mission to help schools in South Africa with the gradual intergration to online learning keeping up with the Iternational standards of the 4th Industrial revolution

Learning styles

We focus on application learning where individuals are required not to cram but understand and be able to apply their knowledge in real life problems.

This is essential in fields such as the medical, civil and software engineering and politics which are all essential in the improvement of a country as a whole
